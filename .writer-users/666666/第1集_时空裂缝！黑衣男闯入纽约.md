# 第1集 时空裂缝！黑衣男闯入纽约

## 中文剧本

【场次1】外景 纽约曼哈顿中城 日
△ 正午的曼哈顿阳光刺眼，高楼之间蛛网般的蛛丝闪过，红蓝战衣的蜘蛛侠拎着三个被捆成粽子的抢劫犯，落在警车旁边。
△ 围观群众发出欢呼，蜘蛛侠对着镜头比了个剪刀手，嘴炮属性全开。
蜘蛛侠（吊儿郎当）：不用谢不用谢，举手之劳，记得给号角报的彼得帕克打赏啊，他拍我照片可是没少挨老板骂。
△ 警察刚要上前道谢，天空突然传来刺耳的撕裂声，半空中裂开一道十米宽的紫色时空裂缝，闪电在裂缝里滋滋作响。
△ 所有人抬头看天，一个全身包裹黑色战甲的身影从裂缝里摔出来，重重砸在对面的帝国大厦顶层，碎石从楼顶滚落。
蜘蛛侠（瞬间收起嬉皮笑脸，按下蛛丝发射器）：哦豁，这下麻烦大了。

【场次2】外景 帝国大厦顶层 日
△ 蝙蝠侠从碎石堆里站起身，头盔上的白色目镜扫过周围的高楼，喉间发出低低的闷哼。
蝙蝠侠（内心OS）：这里不是哥谭。
△ 他抬起手腕启动战甲的侦查模式，蓝色的扫描光线扫过整个城区，数据在目镜上飞速滚动。
△ 嗖的一声，蜘蛛侠落在他对面五米的位置，双手举着蛛丝发射器对准蝙蝠侠。
蜘蛛侠：喂，穿黑衣服的，你是从哪个cosplay会场跑出来的？刚刚那个裂缝是你搞的？你最好给我一个合理的解释，不然我可要把你捆去警察局喝咖啡了。
△ 蝙蝠侠没有说话，手臂一抬，三枚泛着寒光的蝙蝠镖朝着蜘蛛侠飞过去。

【场次3】外景 曼哈顿高空 日
△ 蜘蛛感官瞬间触发，蜘蛛侠猛地弯腰，蝙蝠镖擦着他的头皮飞过去，钉在身后的水泥墙上，没入三分之一。
蜘蛛侠（惊出一身冷汗）：玩真的？！
△ 他抬手射出两道蛛丝黏住蝙蝠镖甩回去，蝙蝠侠侧身躲开，钩爪枪发射勾住楼顶边缘，整个人朝着蜘蛛侠飞踹过来。
△ 两人在半空交手，蜘蛛侠的拳头打在蝙蝠侠的战甲上只发出沉闷的响声，蝙蝠侠的格斗术招招狠辣，逼得蜘蛛侠连连后退。
△ 打了十几个回合不分胜负，蝙蝠侠按下腰带按钮，一团浓烈的烟雾弹炸开。
△ 等烟雾散去，蝙蝠侠已经不见踪影，只有地上残留的一枚蝙蝠镖。
△ 蜘蛛侠蹲下来捡起蝙蝠镖，抬头看向还在缓慢愈合的紫色裂缝，眉头紧锁。
蜘蛛侠（喃喃自语）：这家伙到底是什么来头？
（第一集 完）

## English Script

### SCENE 1

EXT. MIDTOWN MANHATTAN - DAY
△ Bright noon sun shines over Manhattan. Spider-Man, in his red and blue suit, swings between skyscrapers, carrying three robbers tied up in webbing. He lands next to a police car.
△ Crowds cheer. Spider-Man gives a peace sign to the onlookers, quipping as usual.
SPIDER-MAN (casual): No thanks necessary, folks. Just doing my job. And hey, if you see Peter Parker over at the Daily Bugle, slip him a tip, will you? J. Jonah Jameson chews him out for my photos every week.
△ The officer steps forward to thank him, when a sharp tearing sound cuts through the air. A 30-foot purple rift opens in the sky, sparks of lightning crackling inside.
△ Everyone looks up. A figure in full black armor falls through the rift, slamming into the top of the Empire State Building, sending rubble cascading down.
SPIDER-MAN (dropping his joking tone, activating web shooters): Well, that's not good.

### SCENE 2

EXT. EMPIRE STATE BUILDING ROOFTOP - DAY
△ Batman stands up from the rubble, his white cowl lenses scanning the unfamiliar skyline. He grunts softly.
BATMAN (V.O.): This isn't Gotham.
△ He lifts his wrist, activating his suit's scanners. Blue light washes over the city, data scrolling across his lenses.
△ A whoosh sound. Spider-Man lands 15 feet away, web shooters aimed directly at Batman.
SPIDER-MAN: Hey, guy in the batsuit. You escape from a comic con or something? You got anything to do with that weird rift in the sky? You better start talking, or I'm webbing you up and dropping you at NYPD.
△ Batman doesn't respond. He lifts his arm, throwing three sharp batarangs straight at Spider-Man.

### SCENE 3

EXT. MANHATTAN SKY - DAY
△ Spider-sense fires off. Spider-Man ducks hard, the batarangs grazing his head and embedding three inches deep in the concrete wall behind him.
SPIDER-MAN (shocked): Oh, you wanna play rough?
△ He fires two webs, catching the batarangs and flinging them back. Batman dodges, firing his grapple gun to hook the roof edge, launching himself at Spider-Man with a flying kick.
△ They fight mid-air. Spider-Man's punches land with dull thuds against Batman's armor. Batman's hand-to-hand combat is precise and brutal, forcing Spider-Man back.
△ After a dozen evenly matched exchanges, Batman presses a button on his belt. A thick cloud of smoke explodes outward.
△ When the smoke clears, Batman is gone. Only a single batarang lies on the roof.
△ Spider-Man picks up the batarang, looking up at the slowly closing purple rift, frowning.
SPIDER-MAN (muttering): Who the hell is this guy?
(END OF EPISODE 1)
