# 第2集 误会升级！蜘蛛侠VS暗夜义警

## 中文剧本

【场次1】外景 皇后区银行门口 夜
△ 夜晚的皇后区灯光昏暗，银行的警报声尖锐响起，三个蒙面劫匪扛着钱袋从银行里冲出来，刚要上车，一道黑色的身影从天而降。
△ 蝙蝠侠落地的瞬间就放倒了两个劫匪，剩下的一个刚要掏枪，蝙蝠镖精准打飞他的手枪，蝙蝠捆锁瞬间缠住他的手脚，整套动作行云流水，不到10秒解决战斗。
△ 蛛丝荡过来的蜘蛛侠刚好看到这一幕，以为蝙蝠侠是黑吃黑的劫匪同伙，瞬间火冒三丈。
蜘蛛侠：我当是谁呢，原来是个黑吃黑的主儿？穿得人模狗样的，居然抢银行？

【场次2】外景 银行门口 夜
△ 蜘蛛侠抬手就射蛛丝，直奔蝙蝠侠的面门，蝙蝠侠侧身躲开，蛛丝黏在身后的银行柱子上，力道大得把柱子拉出一道裂纹。
蝙蝠侠（沙哑低沉，第一次开口）：让开。
蜘蛛侠（更生气）：哟，还会说话？我还以为你是个哑巴呢！抢完银行还想走？问过我没有！
△ 蜘蛛侠冲上去和蝙蝠侠近身格斗，蝙蝠侠的格斗术招招直奔要害，蜘蛛侠靠着蜘蛛感官勉强躲开，手臂被蝙蝠镖划开一道小口子。
△ 蜘蛛侠抬脚踹向蝙蝠侠的胸口，蝙蝠侠硬抗了这一脚，反手抓住蜘蛛侠的脚踝，把他甩出去十几米远，砸在路边的报刊亭上。

【场次3】外景 高架快速路 夜
△ 蜘蛛侠爬起来追着蝙蝠侠打到高架路上，过往的车辆纷纷急刹车躲避，喇叭声响成一片。
△ 蝙蝠侠按下腰带按钮，一个小型超声波干扰器扔到蜘蛛侠脚边，高频声波瞬间响起。
△ 蜘蛛侠的蜘蛛感官被强烈干扰，抱着头蹲在地上，疼得脸都变形了。
蜘蛛侠（咬牙）：你居然玩阴的？！
△ 蝙蝠侠走到他面前，抬手就要按他的脖子注射镇定剂，蜘蛛侠猛地抬手，两道蛛丝黏住蝙蝠侠的披风，用力一拽。
△ 蝙蝠侠没防备，被拽得向后退了两步，披风被扯下来一大块。

【场次4】外景 高架路边 夜
△ 蝙蝠侠看着自己被扯坏的披风，眼神瞬间冷了下来，他抬起手，腰带上的EMP脉冲装置已经握在手里，发出滋滋的电流声。
△ 蜘蛛侠也从地上爬起来，手腕的蛛丝发射器转到最强模式，额头的伤口流出血迹，眼神丝毫不让。
△ 两人隔着五米的距离对峙，高架路上的车辆都停下了，所有人都看着这两个对峙的超级英雄。
蜘蛛侠（擦了擦嘴角的血）：来啊，我倒要看看你还有多少阴招。
（第二集 完）

## English Script

### SCENE 1

EXT. QUEENS BANK - NIGHT
△ Dim lights cover Queens at night. The bank alarm blares. Three masked robbers run out with duffel bags of cash, just about to get into their getaway car, when a black figure drops from the sky.
△ Batman takes down two robbers the second he lands. The third tries to pull a gun, but a batarang knocks it out of his hand, and bat-cuffs wrap around his wrists and ankles instantly. The whole exchange takes less than 10 seconds.
△ Spider-Man swings over just in time to see the end of the fight, immediately assuming Batman is another robber stealing from the thieves.
SPIDER-MAN: Oh great, so you're the "rob the robbers" type? Fancy suit for a common thief.

### SCENE 2

EXT. BANK ENTRANCE - NIGHT
△ Spider-Man fires a web straight at Batman's face. Batman dodges, the web slamming into the bank column behind him, cracking the concrete.
BATMAN (gravelly, first line of dialogue): Move.
SPIDER-MAN (angrier): Oh, he speaks! I thought you were a mute. You think you're walking away with that money? Not on my watch.
△ Spider-Man lunges into close combat. Batman's fighting style is brutal and precise. Spider-Man barely dodges with his spider-sense, getting a small cut on his arm from a batarang.
△ Spider-Man kicks Batman in the chest. Batman takes the hit, grabbing Spider-Man's ankle and throwing him 50 feet away, crashing into a newsstand.

### SCENE 3

EXT. ELEVATED HIGHWAY - NIGHT
△ Spider-Man gets up, chasing Batman onto the elevated highway. Cars swerve to avoid them, horns blaring.
△ Batman presses a button on his belt, dropping a small sonic disruptor at Spider-Man's feet. High-frequency sound blares.
△ Spider-Man's spider-sense is overwhelmed. He collapses to his knees, clutching his head in pain.
SPIDER-MAN (gritting teeth): Cheap shot!
△ Batman walks over, ready to inject a sedative into Spider-Man's neck, when Spider-Man fires two webs at Batman's cape, yanking hard.
△ Batman is caught off guard, stumbling backward. A large section of his cape tears off.

### SCENE 4

EXT. HIGHWAY SIDE - NIGHT
△ Batman looks at his torn cape, his lenses narrowing. He lifts his hand, an EMP pulse device in his palm, crackling with electricity.
△ Spider-Man gets back to his feet, his web shooters set to maximum power. Blood trickles down his forehead from the newsstand crash, but his gaze doesn't waver.
△ They stand 15 feet apart, facing off. All traffic on the highway has stopped, everyone watching the two vigilantes.
SPIDER-MAN (wiping blood from his lip): Come on. Let's see what else you've got.
(END OF EPISODE 2)
