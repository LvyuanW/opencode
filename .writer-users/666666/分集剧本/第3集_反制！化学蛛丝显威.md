# 第3集 反制！化学蛛丝显威

## 中文剧本

【场次1】内景 彼得的公寓 日
△ 彼得穿着睡衣坐在书桌前，胳膊上的伤口已经包扎好了，桌子上摆着蝙蝠侠的蝙蝠镖碎片和扯下来的披风布料，电脑屏幕上是各种化学分子式。
△ 他拿着镊子夹起披风布料，滴了一滴强酸上去，布料丝毫没有损坏。
彼得（喃喃自语）：凯夫拉加碳纤维复合涂层，还加了抗腐蚀材料，够狠的啊。不过你有张良计，我有过墙梯。
△ 他走到阳台的小实验室里，调配了三种化学试剂，混合之后装进蛛丝发射器的弹药匣里，测试了一下，粘性是普通蛛丝的十倍，还能快速膨胀硬化。
梅婶（画外音）：彼得？下来吃饭了！
彼得（头也不抬）：马上来梅婶！我这有个很重要的实验！
△ 他熬了一整夜，终于把新型化学蛛丝弹药做了出来，眼神里满是兴奋。

【场次2】内景 纽约地铁隧道 夜
△ 蝙蝠侠穿着受损的战甲，在地铁隧道里扫描时空裂缝的能量残留，目镜上的数据显示裂缝的能量正在增强，还检测到了不属于这个宇宙的外星生物信号。
△ 他刚要把数据传送到战甲的云端存储，身后传来蛛丝发射的嗖嗖声，他立刻转身，钩爪枪已经瞄准了声音来源。
△ 蜘蛛侠落在他面前十米的位置，晃了晃手腕上的新蛛丝发射器，脸上带着得意的笑。
蜘蛛侠：蝙蝠老兄，等你好久了。上次的EMP挺好用啊？不过我这次可是专门给你准备了礼物。
△ 蝙蝠侠二话不说，直接扔出EMP脉冲装置，蓝色的电流瞬间炸开，整个隧道的灯都闪了几下。

【场次3】内景 地铁隧道 夜
△ 蜘蛛侠抬起手臂，一层绝缘蛛丝形成的护盾挡在他面前，EMP的电流打在护盾上，连一点火花都没溅起来。
蜘蛛侠：就知道你会来这招，我早把蛛丝发射器做了绝缘改造，你那套不好使了。
△ 他抬手射出两道淡绿色的新型化学蛛丝，蝙蝠侠侧身躲开，蛛丝打在他身后的地铁车身上，瞬间膨胀成半米厚的粘性硬块，牢牢粘住了车身。
△ 蝙蝠侠刚要后退，第二波蛛丝已经到了，直接粘住了他的左臂和战甲的腰部，蛛丝快速硬化，把他整个人牢牢粘在地铁车身上，不管怎么挣扎都挣不开。
蝙蝠侠（难得露出惊讶的语气）：这是什么？
蜘蛛侠（走过去，得意洋洋）：专门为你研发的化学蛛丝，粘性是普通蛛丝的十倍，你那战甲涂层可溶不开它。

【场次4】内景 地铁隧道 夜
△ 蜘蛛侠走到蝙蝠侠面前，伸出手就要摘他的面罩，远处传来地铁的轰鸣声，强风从隧道口吹过来，蝙蝠侠肩膀上附着的一小块黑色共生体组织被风吹起来，粘在了蜘蛛侠的手背上。
△ 蜘蛛侠瞬间感觉手背一阵发麻，像被针扎了一样，他下意识地收回手，低头看自己的手背，那块黑色组织已经消失了，只留下一个小小的黑点。
△ 就在他分神的瞬间，蝙蝠侠抬起右臂，手腕上的微型合金刀片弹出来，快速割开了粘住的战甲外层，挣脱了蛛丝的束缚，钩爪枪发射勾住隧道顶部，整个人荡了出去，刚好落在开过来的地铁顶上，消失在隧道深处。
△ 蜘蛛侠看着自己的手背，又看着蝙蝠侠消失的方向，眉头皱得更紧了。
蜘蛛侠（喃喃自语）：刚才那是什么东西？
（第三集 完）

## English Script

### SCENE 1

INT. PETER'S APARTMENT - DAY
△ Peter sits at his desk in pajamas, his arm bandaged. The batarang fragment and torn cape fabric lie on the desk, chemical formulas scrolling on his computer screen.
△ He dips a drop of strong acid onto the cape fabric. It doesn't even mark the material.
PETER (muttering): Kevlar-carbon fiber composite, corrosion resistant. Smart. But two can play at that game.
△ He goes to his small lab on the balcony, mixing three chemical reagents together. He loads the mixture into his web shooter cartridges, testing it - the stickiness is 10x stronger than normal webbing, and hardens on contact.
AUNT MAY (O.S.): Peter? Dinner's ready!
PETER (not looking up): Coming, Aunt May! Just finishing up an important experiment!
△ He stays up all night, finally finishing the new chemical web cartridges, a grin on his face.

### SCENE 2

INT. NEW YORK SUBWAY TUNNEL - NIGHT
△ Batman, in his damaged suit, scans for energy residue from the space rift in the subway tunnel. His lenses show the rift's energy is increasing, and detect an alien biosignal not from this universe.
△ He's about to upload the data to his cloud storage when he hears the sound of web fire. He turns instantly, grapple gun aimed at the sound.
△ Spider-Man lands 30 feet away, waving his new web shooters with a grin.
SPIDER-MAN: Hey, Bats. Been waiting for you. That EMP trick was cute last time. But I got you a present this time.
△ Batman doesn't respond, throwing an EMP device. Blue electricity explodes, all the tunnel lights flickering.

### SCENE 3

INT. SUBWAY TUNNEL - NIGHT
△ Spider-Man lifts his arm, a shield of insulated webbing blocking the EMP pulse. The electricity hits the shield without even a spark.
SPIDER-MAN: Knew you'd pull that. I insulated my web shooters. Your little tricks don't work anymore.
△ He fires two streams of pale green chemical webbing. Batman dodges, the webbing hitting the subway car behind him, expanding into a two-foot thick hardened mass, stuck firmly to the metal.
△ Batman tries to back up, but a second wave of webbing hits his left arm and waist, hardening instantly. He's stuck tight to the subway car, struggling to break free.
BATMAN (surprised, rare tone): What is this?
SPIDER-MAN (walking toward him, smug): Custom chemical webbing, 10x stickier than normal. Your suit coating can't dissolve it.

### SCENE 4

INT. SUBWAY TUNNEL - NIGHT
△ Spider-Man reaches for Batman's cowl. A subway roars in the distance, strong wind blowing through the tunnel. A small piece of black symbiote tissue on Batman's shoulder blows off, sticking to the back of Spider-Man's hand.
△ Spider-Man feels a sharp stinging sensation, yanking his hand back. The black tissue is gone, only a small dark mark left on his skin.
△ While he's distracted, Batman lifts his right arm, a tiny alloy blade popping out of his gauntlet. He cuts through the outer layer of his suit, breaking free of the webbing. His grapple gun fires, hooking the tunnel ceiling, and he swings onto the roof of the incoming subway, disappearing into the dark tunnel.
△ Spider-Man looks at his hand, then at the direction Batman disappeared, frowning.
SPIDER-MAN (muttering): What the hell was that?
(END OF EPISODE 3)
